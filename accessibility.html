Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our website and products, regardless of ability.

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Our commitment

CI Business Solutions and Analytics is committed to making its website and products accessible to as many people as possible, including people with disability. We recognise our responsibilities under the Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (Cth).

Standard we follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, the internationally recognised standard for web accessibility. We work towards this through practices such as:

  • clear, consistent navigation and page structure;
  • sufficient colour contrast and readable, resizable text;
  • meaningful text alternatives for non-text content;
  • keyboard accessibility for interactive elements; and
  • labels and instructions on forms.

Need content in another format?

If you have difficulty accessing any part of our website or products, or you would like information in an alternative format, please tell us and we will do our best to help.

Feedback and contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website and products. If you encounter a barrier or have suggestions, contact us at operations@cipro.com.au or via our contact page, and we will respond as soon as we can.
