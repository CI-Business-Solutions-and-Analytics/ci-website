Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use our website and products.

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Our commitment

CI Business Solutions and Analytics is committed to making our website and products accessible to as many people as possible, regardless of ability or technology. We aim to align with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

What we do

  • Use clear, readable typography and sufficient colour contrast.
  • Support keyboard navigation and respect reduced-motion preferences.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Structure pages with semantic, screen-reader-friendly markup.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We continue to review and improve our website and products, and we welcome feedback that helps us do better.

Feedback

If you experience any difficulty accessing our website, or have a suggestion, please contact us at operations@cipro.com.au. We will do our best to provide the information or assistance you need in an alternative format.
