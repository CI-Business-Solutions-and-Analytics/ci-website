The problem

The work that shapes your workforce falls through the cracks

Most large organisations have invested heavily in core HR systems — and still can't do the things that matter most for shaping their workforce. Role design, organisational restructuring, and workforce planning typically fall through the cracks: handled in spreadsheets, slide decks, and disconnected tools.

The result is HR technology that's often expensive, cumbersome, and inefficient — strong on records and transactions, weak on the design and planning work that actually shapes an organisation.

Our approach

Augment what works — don't rip and replace

CI delivers focused, cloud-based products that integrate with your existing systems and add the capability they're missing — turning underused workforce data into insight, and one-off restructures into continuous improvement.
