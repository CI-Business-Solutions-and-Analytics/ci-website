Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you use our website and products.

Last updated: 6 June 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how CI Business Solutions and Analytics (ABN 83 645 189 082) ("CI", "we", "us") handles personal information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and to handling personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details you provide through our enquiry form, such as your name, organisation, email address and phone number.
  • Customer data that organisations upload to or process through our products (which may include personal information about their workforce).
  • Technical information such as your device, browser and basic usage data collected when you visit our website.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide demonstrations;
  • provide, support and improve our products and services;
  • communicate with customers about their account and our services; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Customer data

Where an organisation uses our products to process information about its own people, that organisation is responsible for that data and for obtaining any necessary consents. We process such data on their behalf, under our agreement with them, and only as instructed.

5. Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers (for example, hosting and infrastructure providers) who help us deliver our services, and where required or authorised by law.

6. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. See our Security page for more detail on our approach.

7. Access and correction

You may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period.

8. Contact us

If you have a question or complaint about privacy, please contact us at operations@cipro.com.au or by post at 15 Mintbush St, Mambourin VIC 3024, Australia.
