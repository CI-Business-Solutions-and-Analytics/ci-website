Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, and protect personal information — and the rights you have over it.

Last updated: 10 June 2026

CI Business Solutions and Analytics (ABN 83 645 189 082) ("CI", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

We provide cloud-based HR products for role design, organisation design, and workforce planning. In delivering these products we act as a service provider to our customers: where we process employee or workforce data on a customer's behalf, that customer is responsible for how the data is collected and used, and we handle it under our agreement with them.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact and enquiry information — name, email address, phone number, organisation, and the content of messages you send us (for example, via our contact form).
  • Account and customer information — details needed to set up and administer a subscription.
  • Customer-provided data — workforce and HR data that a customer uploads to or processes through our products, which may include personal information about their employees.
  • Technical information — log data, device and browser details, and usage information collected automatically when you use our website or products.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide, operate, and support our products;
  • manage subscriptions, billing, and our relationship with customers;
  • maintain the security, integrity, and performance of our services;
  • comply with our legal obligations; and
  • improve our products and communicate relevant updates (you can opt out of marketing communications at any time).

3. Sensitive information

HR data can include sensitive information. We only collect sensitive information where it is reasonably necessary for our functions and, where required, with consent. Customer-provided data is processed only as needed to deliver the product and under the instructions of the relevant customer.

4. Disclosure of information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose it to:

  • trusted service providers who help us operate our business (for example, hosting, infrastructure, and analytics providers), under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations;
  • professional advisers, where reasonably required; and
  • authorities or other parties where required or authorised by law.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. For details of our technical and organisational measures, see our Security page.

7. Data breaches

We maintain procedures to detect, assess, and respond to data breaches. Where a breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme.

8. Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete. Where the information was provided to us by a customer about their employees, we will direct such requests to that customer.

9. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, after which it is securely deleted or de-identified.

10. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please contact us using the details below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the "last updated" date revised accordingly.

12. Contact us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at operations@cipro.com.au or via our contact page.
