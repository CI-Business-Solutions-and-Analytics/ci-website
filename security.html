Security

How we protect the data organisations trust us with.

Last updated: 6 June 2026

Our approach

Because our products handle workforce and role-related data, security is central to how we build and operate. We take reasonable, industry-aligned steps to protect customer data against misuse, loss, and unauthorised access.

Data protection

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Access to customer data is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • We apply the principle of least privilege across our systems.

Infrastructure

Our products are hosted with reputable cloud infrastructure providers that maintain industry-recognised security certifications. We keep systems patched and monitored.

Operational practices

  • Regular review of access and permissions.
  • Backups to support recovery and continuity.
  • Staff awareness of privacy and security responsibilities.

Reporting a concern

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability or have a security concern, please contact us at operations@cipro.com.au. We take all reports seriously and will respond promptly.
